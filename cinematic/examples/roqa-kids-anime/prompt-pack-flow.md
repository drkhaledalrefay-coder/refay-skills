# Ruqayya and the Kitten — Google Flow prompt pack

A 60-second kids anime about a little girl who gives her water to a thirsty kitten. Copy a lock, then a shot. Make the pictures first. Animate them after the stills look right.

قصة قصيرة لرقية والقطة العطشانة. انسخي القفل، ثم لقطة لقطة.

The hadith card at the end is made in the edit, not in Flow. Check the wording and the source before it goes on screen: «في كل ذات كبد رطبة أجر».

## Locks

**Style**

```
Widescreen 16:9. Premium hand-painted Japanese animated feature-film aesthetic. Pure daylight, warm sun rays filtering through trees, fluffy clouds, bright summer sky, lush detailed painted backgrounds. No text or subtitles.
```

**Ruqayya**

```
Ruqayya, a 7-year-old Egyptian girl (exact same cute round face, braids, yellow T-shirt, blue denim overalls, and crossbody satchel)
```

**Kitten**

```
a tiny orange tabby kitten with white paws and a white chest, big green eyes, thin and tired
```

**Village**

```
A hot summer noon in an Egyptian countryside village: dusty stone path beside a low mud-brick wall, a few palm trees, green fields and mountains in the distance, fluffy clouds, strong sunlight with soft shade.
```

**Audio ending (every video)**

```
No music. No text or subtitles.
```

Describe the girl by hair, clothes, and a cute round face. Skip body descriptions.

## How to attach pictures

For video, use Reference-to-Video. Type @ and pick three images: her character sheet, the kitten sheet, and that shot's keyframe. Say which is which in the first line.

First-frame mode takes a single image. The face can drift. The clips below from the kitten onward use Reference-to-Video.
