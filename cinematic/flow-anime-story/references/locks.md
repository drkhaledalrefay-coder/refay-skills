# Paste-ready locks (Flow)

Use the v0.3 set. The v0.1 set is only for a "what failed" explanation.

## STYLE LOCK (works)

```
Widescreen 16:9. Premium hand-painted Japanese animated feature-film aesthetic. Pure daylight, warm sun rays filtering through trees, fluffy clouds, bright summer sky, lush detailed painted backgrounds. No text or subtitles.
```

Short form used inside shot prompts:

```
Widescreen 16:9. Premium hand-painted Japanese animated feature-film aesthetic.
```

## CHARACTER LOCK — Ruqayya

```
Ruqayya, a 7-year-old Egyptian girl (exact same cute round face, braids, yellow T-shirt, blue denim overalls, and crossbody satchel)
```

Age wording that passed the filter: "7-year-old" and "a cheerful little schoolgirl, about 6 years old". Failed wording: body proportions, a missing tooth. Do not describe a child's body. Hair, clothes, bag, and a cute round face are enough.

Props that stay in shot prompts when needed: blue water bottle, small lunchbox lid, brown crossbody satchel.

## KITTEN LOCK

```
a tiny orange tabby kitten with white paws and a white chest, big green eyes, thin and tired
```

If a keyframe looks angry or hissing, the video line that fixed it: sad and weak, not angry. Say the positive: "sad and weak, soft gentle face, eyes half closed."

## LOCATION LOCK (v0.3, the one that shipped)

```
Egyptian countryside village: dusty stone path beside a low mud-brick wall, a few palm trees, green fields and mountains in the distance, fluffy clouds, strong sunlight with soft shade
```

Earlier village-valley wording (stream, hydrangeas, water wheel, rice terraces) was drafted and not the look of the finished keyframes. Use the mud-brick wall version.

## AUDIO LOCK

```
Audio: <one ambience list>. No music. No text or subtitles.
```

Speech, only when the shot needs it:

```
She whispers in sweet, natural Egyptian Arabic: "<short line>".
```

S07 shipped with "No music, no speech."
