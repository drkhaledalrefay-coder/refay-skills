# Brand Campaign Planner

Plan a digital campaign for a brand in its actual market, using the brand's voice, visual rules and local constraints.

## At a glance

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Use it for** | Use for campaign strategy, calendars, platform-native creative, creator/UGC briefs, e-commerce copy or claims review. |
| **You need** | Brand/product materials, target market and audience, objectives, channels, timing and available compliance rules. |
| **It produces** | A campaign plan and requested content, with an intake record and source-backed claims/market adaptation. |

## How it works

Builds a brand dossier; asks about market, language, culture and commerce; defines direction; creates channel-specific content; checks claims and disclosures.

## What it does not do

Does not assume a market by default. Missing market facts, claims and regulatory rules must be confirmed rather than invented.
