# Phase 0 — Intake questionnaire

Ask in rounds of 3–4 questions. Pre-fill answers you can infer from files the user already pointed to and ask them to confirm instead of asking from scratch. Save the answers to `brief.json`.

## Round 1 — Market & people
1. **Country / city (or region)** where the videos will run and be set. (Drives dialect, faces, homes, climate.)
2. **Language + dialect** of the spoken script (e.g. a regional dialect vs. the standard language, or US/UK English). Who will review native copy?
3. **Cultural & modesty rules** for on-screen people (e.g. head covering fully hiding hair and hairline, long sleeves to the wrist, loose clothing; or none). Anything forbidden?
4. **Target audience & socioeconomic level** (e.g. "middle-class to affluent women 18–35"). Sets homes, cars, clothing quality.

## Round 2 — Campaign
5. **Number of videos** — suggest grid multiples (3/6/9/12) so the profile grid is complete. One product per video? Repeats allowed?
6. **Products** — list, with any priority. For each: exact SKU/size. (Watch for sibling SKUs: "<PRODUCT> Gel" ≠ "<PRODUCT> Gel Sensitive"; "<PRODUCT> Balm" ≠ "<PRODUCT> Body Lotion".)
7. **Platforms & format** — TikTok / Reels / Snapchat / Shorts; 9:16; target length per video (default 15–24 s, 2–3 clips of ~8 s).
8. **Tone / campaign idea** — existing campaign name/hashtag? Disclosure rules (e.g. "#إعلان", "in partnership with")?

## Round 3 — Sources & tools
9. **Brand guide** location (colors, fonts, logo, casing rules).
10. **Claims sources** — training PDFs, product sheets, clinical studies; which are approved for public use?
11. **Packshots** folder(s). Missing ones → placeholder, never substitute.
12. **Image & video tools** — e.g. Nano Banana / GPT Image / Midjourney for stills; Google Flow / Veo / Kling / Higgsfield for video. (Prompt syntax adapts.) Will the user generate, or should you use an available generation API (costs credits — confirm first)?
13. **Output folder** and file naming preference.

## Round 4 — Process
14. **Approval gates** — default: after every phase. Who approves?
15. Anything from past campaigns to keep or avoid?

## Defaults if the user says "you decide"
- 9 videos (3×3 grid), 2–3 clips each, 16–24 s.
- Personas: one per video, ages spread 18–35, mix of occupations, all from the market's region.
- Deck in English with native-language scripts; brand colors; Dubai for Arabic.
